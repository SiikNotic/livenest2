TikTok Live Alerts

Configure TikTok Live alerts with LiveNest. Announce gifts, new followers and chat activity with sound and voice.

Alerts help creators notice important moments during a live stream. A new follower, a gift or a meaningful interaction can be easy to miss when chat is moving quickly.

LiveNest includes an activity view and an alert system for organizing these events. You can choose which alert channels to use and configure the behavior for different event types.

For busy streams, it helps to separate normal chat messages from important events. This lets voice reading focus on the information you actually need to hear.

Alerts live alongside chat reading, filters and music tools, so you can manage the stream from one environment instead of constantly switching between separate tools.

Events you can manage

  • New followers.
  • Gifts.
  • Likes and shares.
  • Subscriptions.
  • Chat messages.
  • Sound and voice announcements based on your settings.
