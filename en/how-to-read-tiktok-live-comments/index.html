How to Read TikTok Live Comments Aloud

A practical guide to reading TikTok Live comments aloud with LiveNest, including voice settings, filters and live event alerts.

Reading TikTok Live chat while you are talking, gaming or making music can be difficult. A voice reader turns written audience messages into audio so you can follow the conversation without constantly looking away from your stream.

With LiveNest, open the tool, sign in, enter the username of the channel that is live and connect. When messages arrive, the app can read them aloud according to your settings.

Before starting a live, configure your chat filters. You can reduce repetitive messages, spam or content you do not want to hear. You can also choose which live events should be announced with sound or voice.

LiveNest includes reading and voice controls so you can adapt the experience to the type of stream you run.

Quick setup

  • Open LiveNest in your browser or on Android.
  • Sign in or create an account.
  • Enter the TikTok Live channel username.
  • Connect while the channel is live.
  • Configure voice, reading and filters.
  • Start your stream and let LiveNest read the chat.
