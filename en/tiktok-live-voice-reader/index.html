TikTok Live Voice Reader

Read TikTok Live comments aloud with LiveNest. Configure text-to-speech, chat filters, gift and follower alerts from web or Android.

LiveNest is a tool for TikTok Live creators who want to hear their chat without manually reading every message. Connect your channel while it is live and use voice reading to stay focused on your audience and your stream.

LiveNest can also announce live events such as new followers, gifts, likes, shares and subscriptions. You can choose which events should be announced and configure the experience around your stream.

Chat filters help reduce messages you do not want to hear, while reading controls let you adapt the experience to the pace of your live. LiveNest is designed for the web and Android.

The experience is bilingual in Spanish and English, making it useful for creators whose communities communicate in both languages.

What can you do with LiveNest?

  • Read TikTok Live comments aloud.
  • Configure voice and reading options.
  • Filter spam, emoji-heavy messages and unwanted text.
  • Announce gifts, followers and other live events.
  • Let viewers request music through chat.
  • Manage saved channels and preferences from your account.
